sp_parkmusic - gentle music around Legion Square park
======================================================

1. Put the sp_parkmusic folder in your resources (fxmanifest.lua directly inside it).
2. Add to server.cfg:   ensure sp_parkmusic
3. Restart. No database, no dependencies.

How it works
- When a player spawns / loads their character, the music slowly fades in (8s by default)
  if they are at Legion Square park.
- Volume follows distance: full (but soft) volume inside the park, drifting to silent
  as they walk away. Audio is fully paused when out of range, so it costs nothing.
- Each player can toggle it with /parkmusic (remembered between sessions).

Music
- Out of the box it plays generated calm ambient music (soft pads + chimes), no files needed.
- To use your own music: copy .ogg or .mp3 files into html/music/ and list the file
  names in Config.Tracks in config.lua. They play shuffled on a loop.
  Only use music you have the rights to play.

Tweak position, radius, volume and fade times in config.lua.
