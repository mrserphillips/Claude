V4.4 POPS & BANGS + COLOURED FLAMES

POPS & BANGS (overrun crackle)
- After lifting off the throttle at speed, the exhaust keeps crackling with
  irregular pops and occasional bangs/mega bangs while you coast.
- Starts only from a real lift-off (not from rolling with no input), stops when you
  get back on the throttle, drop below Config.PopsBangs.MinSpeedKmh, or after
  Config.PopsBangs.MaxDurationMs.
- Not every pop spits a flame (Config.PopsBangs.FlameChance); bangs always do.
- Can be switched on/off per vehicle, saved to the plate.
- Existing limiter and lift-off bursts are unchanged.

COLOURED FLAMES
- Presets: Stock, Blue, Cyan, Green, Purple, Pink, Red, White hot, Rainbow.
- Custom colour picker (any hex colour) - toggle with Config.AllowCustomColour.
- Colour is saved per plate and synced, so everyone nearby sees the same colour.
- Add/remove/edit presets in Config.FlameColours.
- Tint is applied to GTA's own veh_backfire flame, so bright saturated colours look best.

MENU
- Sit in the driver seat of a fitted vehicle and type /antilag
  (command name: Config.MenuCommand).
- Config.SettingsPermission = 'driver' (anyone driving it) or 'mechanic' (mechanics only).
- The server re-checks the driver seat, the plate and the colour value.

DATABASE
- Two new columns: flame_colour, pops_bangs. Added automatically on start,
  existing fitted plates are kept (they get Stock colour + pops & bangs ON).

OTHER
- /removeantilag now uses Config.RemoveTime instead of a hard-coded 10 seconds.
