V4.1 NATIVE EFFECTS TEST
- Removed NUI/browser WAV playback completely.
- Pops/bangs are now GTA native positional sounds emitted from the vehicle entity.
- No prerecorded multi-pop audio sequence.
- Flames use networked veh_backfire directly on exhaust bones.
- Addon fallback remains if the vehicle exposes no exhaust bones.
- Install, persistence and removal from V4 are retained.
- install.sql retained.

IMPORTANT:
This build intentionally uses existing GTA soundsets rather than pretending a custom AWC exists.
A genuinely custom native wavepack requires authored .awc + .dat54.rel metadata; those binaries are not fabricated here.
