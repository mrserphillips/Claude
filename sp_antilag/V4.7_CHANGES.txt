V4.7
SOUND
- Pops, bangs and megas restored to the V4.4.4 sound (the first preview, "v1").
- BIG bang kept, rebuilt to match v1: hard crack, low 320Hz rumble, two overdriven tones
  dropping 95->25Hz, a 58->20Hz sub drop and v1's long street echo.
- Where BIG bangs happen is unchanged from V4.6 (end of limiter/lift bursts, crackle finale,
  4% mid-crackle) - see Config.BigBang and the sequences.

COLOURED FLAMES
- Firework effects removed (they spat stars). Config.ColourFxOptions is now flames only:
  nitrous exhaust flame (default), nitrous + stock, jet afterburner, fire jet (two aims),
  tinted backfire one-shot / looped.
- /antilagfx cycles them in your colour; set Config.ColourFx to the one that works.
- A custom recoloured flame particle (.ypt) streamed on the server can be added as an option.
