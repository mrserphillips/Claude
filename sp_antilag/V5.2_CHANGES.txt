V5.2 - Supra-style anti-lag rattle
- Pops and bangs are now an anti-lag rattle ("brrrap") tuned against a Supra anti-lag
  recording: each shot plays 3-5 (pop) or 5-8 (bang) micro-hits 13-35ms apart with +-2dB
  level jitter, peaking at 150-250Hz with a crisp rattle band at 2.5-6kHz.
- Holding the limiter (2-step shots every 130-180ms) now runs together into one continuous
  brrrrap at ~40 hits a second, like the recording. Overrun crackle gives separate braps.
- The micro-hits are played by each client from the one networked shot, so network
  traffic is unchanged. Mega and BIG bangs keep the gunshot sound.

V5.2.1
- Rattle about 6dB louder (RATTLE_GAIN in html/index.html).
- Loud accent bangs inside the rattle: 8% of pop micro-hits, 18% of bang micro-hits hit
  at 2x level with a thump under them.
- More bangs: overrun crackle BangChance 0.30 -> 0.40, MegaChance 0.15 -> 0.18; 2-step mix
  shifted towards bang/mega at every intensity.

V5.2.2
- BIG bangs fire 3 times back to back like gunshots (Config.BigBang.Count / GapMs). Applies
  everywhere a BIG bang fires: crackle finale, lift-off and gear change bursts, Maximum 2-step.
- Loud bangs inside the rattle are much louder (~12dB above the rattle instead of ~7dB): each
  is a hit at 3.6x plus a dry gunshot crack (ACCENT_LEVEL / ACCENT_SHOT in html/index.html).
  They are rarer so they stay a few big hits (4% of pop micro-hits, 9% of bang micro-hits).
- To make room for them the everyday rattle sits ~3dB lower (RATTLE_GAIN 1.8 -> 0.75): the
  NUI audio has a hard ceiling and the rattle was already pinned against it. Raise
  RATTLE_GAIN for a louder but flatter rattle, or turn up Config.SoundVolume / game volume.
